Sihyun Kim

Los Angeles Chapter President

Hello I'm Sihyun Kim, leading the Los Angeles chapter of Hearts for Healing. I'm dedicated to spreading compassion and care throughout our vibrant city.

Under my leadership, our chapter has expanded to serve multiple hospitals and community centers across LA. I specialize in creating intergenerational programs that bring together youth and seniors.

My background in psychology helps me understand the emotional needs of those we serve, ensuring our programs have maximum impact. In my free time, I enjoy painting and writing poetry.
